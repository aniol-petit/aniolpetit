How I helped my friend getting an internship at REVER

← Back
4th April, 2025

Friday morning, I get a text from my friend Jan: the start-up where he applied for an internship was hosting a mini-hackathon that night, the winner of the competition would get the position. He didn’t tell me much more, other than he could bring a friend to the challenge to help him (that was me), and that there’d be beers and shots. He also showed me this video, which was presented by REVER as what to expect from the night: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rJjxH_2OuKw&ab_channel=LegendaryMovieScenes

That video, and the idea of helping one of my best friends getting the internship he’d been talking about for some time, was more than enough to convince me not to go back to my town that afternoon as I’d normally do on a random Friday and stay in Barcelona for the special occasion.

We were scheduled at 19:30 and it was supposed to last until 22:30, 3 hours of intense coding (or that we thought). We got there half an hour earlier and, together with the other participants, we got a tour around the offices: see the space, meet the team, and a small chat with the founders took almost two hours of time, so I was basically wondering: “when exactly are we going to start hacking?”

So it’s around 21:00 and we finally got to go to the upper floor, where the competition would take place, and we find a table full (check the images) of alcohol bottles, beers and energetics. Things were getting serious. And when we thought we were ready to hear what would the competition be about, the REVER team tells us to start drinking, chatting and getting to know each other. As someone who had little stake in that competition, I must admit I was pretty excited about that. So after half an hour of laughs and more than a drink, the challenge started: there were several rounds, the last team to complete the task of a specific round would be out, until there’d be only 1 remaining.

Things started smoothly, Jan and I make a pretty good team so we went past the first 2 rounds quite easily. Things got messy when we had to buy time for the rounds by drinking shots (1 shot = 1 minute)…those Tequila shots combined with the 3 drinks I had on me left me wasted for some time, leaving Jan alone for the 3rd and part of the 4th round (I’m still sorry for that). But since I knew he was more than capable of keep passing rounds (which he did) I decided to focus on getting back on my feet and be ready for the last rounds.

After a miraculous recovery and thanks to Jan’s skills, we were both alive to tackle the last round. It was around 3a.m., the first team to deliver the right solution won the competition and got the internship, and if you read until here you can imagine what happened…WE WON!! We were probably the most drunk couple, for sure not the best hackers in the room, but we had something different: a very strong connection, outstanding teamwork, and the greatest determination to win the competition. I have to give credit for that last thing to Jan, who is the most competitive person I know, and that spirit was key to get that win. But the biggest takeaway from that day, rather than the big fun we had or the win itself, was the lesson I was shown: in life it’s not only about how good you are doing something, but if you are determined enough and have good people to work with around you, you can achieve much more than you could possibly imagine.
