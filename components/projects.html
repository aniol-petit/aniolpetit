Projects

Oralio

We created a start-up with college colleagues to create AI-powered solutions for patients and dental professionals. Currently we are focusing on braces tracking and launched My Braces Tracker, available on the App Store and Google Play.

FlowGuard

Developped an AI model to detect and explain the risk of failure of smart water meters in Barcelona and displayed results in an interactive webapp to prioritize maintenance. Won the 1st award (6.000€) in the Aigües de Barcelona Data Challenge.

AI Search Visibility Trust Layer

Built a fully containerised stack (Docker + Groq Llama-3) to score brand visibility in AI search, with results in a React dashboard. Reverse-engineered model behaviour by correlating LLM outputs with domain authority, GitHub stars, and competitor overlap to see how positioning shapes recommendations.

Image Captioning

We extended an open-source CNN-RNN image captioning model from GitHub by adding visual attention for interpretability and top-p sampling for diverse outputs. We also developed a UI to test captions and visualize attention maps.
